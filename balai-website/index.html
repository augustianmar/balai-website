The commercial-access gap

Potential is easy to see.
A reliable route to business is harder to build.

Cross-market opportunities often stall not because interest is missing, but because the right context, partners and commercial follow-through are not yet in place.

Context does not become a commercial decision

Cultural knowledge becomes commercially useful only when it shapes target segments, positioning, products and outreach.

The right partners are difficult to qualify

A long contact list is not commercial access. Companies need relevant, qualified and realistically compatible partners.

03

Trust grows through preparation, communication and follow-up—not through a single email or trade-fair meeting.
