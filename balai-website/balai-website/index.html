THE COMMERCIAL-ACCESS GAP

Demand can exist long before direct access does.

Many tourism companies already serve Southeast Asian customers indirectly, yet lack visibility into the markets, partners and purchasing structures behind that demand.

FIN↔SEA
01

The source market remains hidden

International wholesalers and intermediary chains can obscure where customers originate and who influences the purchase.

02

Relevant partners are difficult to qualify

A long contact list is not market access. Companies need compatible travel-trade partners, suppliers and decision-makers.

03

Introductions are not developed into business

Commercial relationships require preparation, market-specific communication and consistent follow-through.
