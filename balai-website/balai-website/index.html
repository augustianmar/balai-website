The market-access gap

Potential is easy to see.
Access is harder to build.

Most cross-market projects do not fail because the opportunity is invisible. They stall because local context, partner quality and commercial continuity are missing.

Context is not translated into decisions

Cultural knowledge only becomes commercially useful when it shapes target segments, positioning, packages and outreach.

02

The right partners are difficult to identify

A long contact list is not market access. Companies need relevant, qualified and realistically compatible partners.

03

Introductions are not carried through

Trust grows through preparation, communication and follow-up—not through a single email or trade-fair meeting.
