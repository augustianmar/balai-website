The commercial gap

Southeast Asian demand exists. Capturing it is the difficult part.

A destination can be attractive without being commercially accessible. BALAI helps close the gap between interest in Lapland and the practical work required to turn that interest into qualified partners, bookings and long-term market presence.

01

The market is treated as one audience

Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore differ in travel behaviour, sales channels, culture, purchasing power and expectations. Generic “Asia marketing” wastes budget.

02

Relationships are missing

Many companies know the market looks promising but do not know which operators, agencies, communities or decision-makers are worth approaching.

03

The offer is not localised

Strong Finnish products can still be difficult to sell when packages, messaging, service expectations and practical details do not fit the buyer.

04

Follow-up disappears

Trade fairs and introductions create little value without structured follow-up, market representation and consistent commercial development.
